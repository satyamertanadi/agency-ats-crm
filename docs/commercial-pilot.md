# Commercial pilot pack

The contract/order form should state:

- One dedicated managed deployment for six named client users: one Owner and five Consultants; one additional named vendor support administrator.
- A 30-day paid production pilot with manual invoicing and no automatic renewal or in-app subscription charge.
- Included onboarding, migration rehearsal, final cutover, Google identity, one-way Calendar, support channel, response targets, and maintenance window.
- Source-file and document-manifest responsibilities, data-quality assumptions, reconciliation approval, and treatment of rejected or omitted records.
- Data controller/processor roles, DPA, subprocessors, region, security measures, privacy notice, retention/deletion, breach handling, and lawful candidate-consent responsibilities.
- Client ownership of its data, export format/timing, termination assistance, post-termination deletion, and legal-hold exceptions.
- Named-user/account-sharing prohibition and the client's responsibility to notify role/offboarding changes promptly.
- Pilot exclusions: Gmail sync, two-way Calendar, autonomous AI decisions, job boards, client accounts, payroll/temp staffing, and subscription billing.

Suggested support priorities: severity 1 for confirmed broad unavailability or active security exposure; severity 2 for a blocked core recruitment workflow without a safe workaround; severity 3 for degraded/non-core behavior. Exact response targets and service hours must be commercially agreed rather than implied by the software.

Pilot acceptance requires all six users operating, demonstrated Calendar reliability, completed runbook exercises, no unresolved severity-1/2 incidents, and written client acceptance after 30 days.
