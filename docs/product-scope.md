# Product scope

The first release is a dedicated managed deployment for one agency: one client owner, five consultants, and one additional named vendor support administrator. Any specifically invited verified Gmail or Google Workspace address may join.

The supported workflow is company → contact → job → candidate → pipeline → submission → client feedback → interview → offer → placement → consultant credit/invoice. Candidates may exist without a job. The ATS is the source of truth for one-way Google Calendar events.

The pilot includes full structured Excel/CSV migration, optional document ZIP/manifest handling as an operator-assisted step, manual commercial invoicing, named-user support, and human review at every consequential decision.

The pilot excludes Gmail inbox sync, Google-to-ATS Calendar updates, autonomous candidate decisions, scraping, job boards, temporary staffing/payroll, client user accounts, semantic search, subscription billing, and public self-service organization creation.
