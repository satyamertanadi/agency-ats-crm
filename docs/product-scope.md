# Product scope

The product connects agency delivery and client development through one workflow: company, contact, job, sourcing, longlist, shortlist, submission, review, interview, offer, placement, guarantee, and fee tracking. Candidates may exist without a job.

The MVP includes organization onboarding, RBAC, candidates, client CRM, jobs, configurable pipelines, submissions, public review links, activities, tasks, interviews, offers, placements, structured search, CSV portability, and explainable CV-assisted evaluation.

Deferred features include live mailbox sync, client accounts, semantic search, subscription billing, job-board integrations, payroll, temporary staffing, scraping, and autonomous candidate rejection.
